Where AI fits

AI does the reading and drafting. People make the calls.

Every stage has a clear owner. AI prepares the work so the owner spends their time on judgment, not on retyping, matching and chasing.

  1. 01

    Inquiry

    AIReads inbound forms and emails, sorts them by language and request type.

    Human checkpointAssigns an owner and decides on next steps.

  2. 02

    Engagement

    AIAssembles the fee schedule and contract from approved templates.

    Human checkpointApproves any pricing exception before it is sent.

  3. 03

    Payment

    AIReads payment proofs (amount, date, payer) and matches them to fee lines.

    Human checkpointConfirms the reconciliation.

  4. 04

    Case work

    AIExtracts data from client documents and tracks every deadline.

    Human checkpointThe responsible professional reviews the work.

  5. 05

    Reports & outreach

    AIDrafts reports and newsletters, flags claims to fact-check, prepares translations.

    Human checkpointApproves before anything leaves the firm.

AI preparesA person approves
How we work
